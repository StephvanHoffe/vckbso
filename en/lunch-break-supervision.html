Lunch-break supervision for schools Sporty coaches in your school playground!

Our coaches are also available during school breaks. Their enthusiasm, youthful energy and experience in working with children are just what a playground needs.

Request an introductory meeting

Break time is when children get to burn off some energy. It’s an important moment for teachers too: time to have lunch in peace with colleagues and catch their breath. Steering all that energy in the right direction and getting children moving? That’s exactly what our coaches are good at.

Just the way your school wants it

We tailor our lunch-break supervision to what your school needs. Common options include:

  • All-round coaches

    Our coaches keep an eye on the whole playground, get children moving, put out any little fires and make sure everything runs smoothly.

  • Sporty programme

    We magically turn the school playground into a Sporty playground. With our own Sporty programme, we set up a range of games and run them. That way, there’s a game for every child to enjoy.

  • The Sporty Break

    We turn it into a real Sporty Break! Our coaches have lunch with the children in the classroom and then run a matching outdoor programme. That way, the teachers get the whole break off.

All our coaches have a certificate of good conduct (VOG) and the right qualifications.

We’re happy to adapt to whatever your school would like.
