Work at Sporty Join the team!

Ever wanted to get paid for running around, laughing and hanging out with friends? Here’s your chance!

As a Sporty coach it’s all about fun, friendship and growth, just like it is for the children. You’ll join a close-knit team that’s a real group of friends. We get up to all sorts together outside working hours too.

Apply now

What does a coach do?

As a coach, you’re responsible for your group. You pick the children up from school, come up with the activities and lead them, and make sure everyone has a brilliant afternoon. You get plenty of responsibility and loads of room for your own ideas. Want to keep growing? You can do that at Sporty too.

Are we a match?

Great with children, responsible and love being on the move? Then we’re looking for you!

Don’t have all the qualifications yet? No problem. Together we’ll check whether you have the right ones. If not, we’ll work out a route together to get them.
