How we work it out

This is an estimate based on the official 2026 table from the Benefits Agency. The Dutch Tax Administration (Belastingdienst) sets your actual benefit. You get the benefit for the months in which you and your benefit partner work, and only for childcare listed in the Dutch National Childcare Register (Landelijk Register Kinderopvang). Want to be sure? Do a trial calculation with the Benefits Agency (in Dutch).

The Sporty Membership

You choose fixed days each week, and your child can come to Sporty all year round: 52 weeks, including school holidays and study days (when school is closed for teacher training).

We move with you

  • On study days your child is always welcome, even if it’s not their usual day (if there’s space).
  • School strike? We’ve got you covered then too.
  • Can’t make a day? Just swap it for another day in the same school week.
  • In the school holidays you pick the days, and we bring the party. We go all out with special activities and games.

What does it cost?

Our hourly rate is €10.31. That’s exactly the maximum hourly rate the government covers, so you get childcare benefit on the full amount. And surprise extra costs? We don’t do those. Food, drink, play equipment and all the specials are simply included.

The same amount every month

On school days your child comes for an afternoon, and on study days and in the holidays for a whole day. That’s why we work with an average over the whole year. With 1 fixed day a week, that’s 27.5 hours a month. So you pay the same every month, with no ups and downs.

Childcare benefit

You get a big chunk back

Good news: you get a large part of the costs back through childcare benefit. How much depends on your income.

  1. Sign your child up with Sporty.
  2. Apply for the benefit at toeslagen.nl (in Dutch) within 3 months of the childcare starting. You’ll need our LRK number (Dutch National Childcare Register); we’ll put it here as soon as we’re listed in the register.
  3. You receive the benefit every month.
