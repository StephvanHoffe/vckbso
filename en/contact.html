Frequently asked questions

Ask Henny (or read it here)

About Sporty

Does my child need to be good at sport?

No, not at all. You really don’t have to be the next Ronaldo. Everyone is welcome, whatever their level and whatever sport they love.

What kinds of sports and games do you play?

All sorts! And don’t worry: we do a lot more than just football. Think tag, ball games and all kinds of other sports and games. Children can also come up with their own ideas, and we always listen.

How big are the groups, and how many coaches are there?

We work with one regular group of up to 20 children. We always stick to the legal staff-to-child ratio: roughly one coach for every ten children, and there are always at least two adults around.

What happens if my child gets hurt?

There’s always at least one adult with a valid paediatric first aid certificate with the group.

An afternoon at Sporty

What are your opening hours, and how late can I pick up my child?

On school days we’re open from the end of the school day; you pick up your child by 18:30 at the latest. On study days (when school is closed for teacher training) we’re open from 08:00 to 18:30. You can reach us by phone from 9:00 to 13:30.

Can I pick up my child earlier?

Yes, that’s possible by arrangement.

How do I let you know my child is ill or won’t be coming?

Via WhatsApp (opens WhatsApp) or email (info@bsosporty.nl). You can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours in advance.

How do you keep me updated?

Day-to-day communication goes through our WhatsApp. On top of that, you’ll get our newsletter and other important information by email.

How do school holidays and study days work?

During the school holidays and on study days we’re open from 08:00 to 18:30. That’s when we run a special Sporty programme packed with fun activities. Read more about holidays and study days.

What do the children get to eat?

The children get sandwiches for lunch. There’s always fruit, and we drink water.

My child has an allergy or a special diet. Is that OK?

Yes, of course. Just mention it during the intake and we’ll take it into account.

Can my child be taken to their sports club?

Yes, that’s possible. Let us know when you sign up and we’ll look at the options together.

What do you do when the weather’s bad?

Then we head indoors and play games there. Or we get creative and turn the place into a cinema, for example.

What does my child need to bring?

Nothing special, just a big dose of energy. We’ll burn that off together. Special trainers or sports kit aren’t needed. But they’re welcome, of course.

Signing up and making changes

Can my child do a trial day first?

Yes, absolutely! Book a free trial day.

How do I sign up my child?

You can sign up using the sign-up form. There’s no obligation at all: we’ll get in touch with you within 24 hours.

How does the first day go?

Your child’s mentor welcomes them personally and shows them around. Halfway through the day, we’ll let you know how it’s going. Be ready to pick up your child after half a day, if needed.

Can I book an extra day or swap a day?

Yes, you can. You can arrange an extra day via WhatsApp (opens WhatsApp) or email. That’s possible if there’s room in the group, and you’ll always get a confirmation. A day not working out this time? Then swap it for another day in the same school week.

How do I change or cancel my child’s place?

Changes and cancellations are done in writing, by email: info@bsosporty.nl. The notice period is 1 month.

What’s the notice period?

The notice period is 1 month, counted from the date of your email.

Costs and admin

How much does BSO Sporty cost?

With the Sporty membership (52 weeks), our hourly rate is €9.75, with no extra costs. Does your child just join in now and then? Then the flexible rate is €12 per hour. You’ll find all the details and a cost calculator under Membership and costs.

How do I pay my invoice?

You’ll get an invoice every month, afterwards. You can pay by direct debit or by bank transfer.

How do I apply for childcare benefit (kinderopvangtoeslag)?

You can apply for childcare benefit via www.toeslagen.nl. Do it within three months of the childcare starting, or you’ll miss out on benefit. Want to know how much childcare benefit you’ll get? Do a trial calculation on www.toeslagen.nl, or use our cost calculator.

Stuck? We’re happy to help. You can reach us Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 13:30 on +31 6 18 26 67 06.

What is Sporty’s LRK number?

Our LRK number is 236315389. It means we’re listed in the Dutch National Childcare Register (Landelijk Register Kinderopvang). You’ll need it when you apply for childcare benefit.

Complaints and feedback

Not happy about something? We’d like to hear about it. Talk to your child’s mentor or to Tim and Sureyo, or submit a formal complaint. Read our complaints procedure.
