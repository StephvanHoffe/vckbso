Why BSO VCK?

Three reasons to choose us

Our full English website is on its way. Here's the short version.

Genuine attention for every child

Every child is different, and we love that. We take the time to listen and really look at what your child needs.

Lots of sports and exercise

Running, jumping, ball games and playing outside. We encourage children to move, at their own level and above all with lots of fun.

Cosy, like a second home

Familiar faces, friends and a warm atmosphere make BSO VCK a place your child loves to go to.
