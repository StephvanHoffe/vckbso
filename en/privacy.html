Privacy

Privacy statement

We take good care of your data. Here’s exactly how.

This is an English translation for your convenience. The Dutch version is the official one: if the two differ, the Dutch version applies.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Who are we?

Sporty is responsible for processing personal data as described in this privacy statement. You can reach us at info@bsosporty.nl or on +31 6 18 26 67 06.

What data do we collect through the website?

If you fill in the contact form, we receive your name, your email address, your phone number (if you fill it in) and your message. We only use this data to answer your question and get in touch with you.

To send the contact form, we use an external form service. That service only processes your data on our behalf.

Applying for a job

Applying through the Become a Sporty coach page? Then we receive your name, email address, phone number, your CV and your cover letter. We only use this data to assess your application and get in touch with you.

Your application comes into our own admin system, not to an external service. We store your CV and cover letter encrypted, and only the colleagues who handle applications can see them. You’ll get a confirmation by email; the notification to our team contains no personal data.

If there’s no place for you, we delete your data no later than 4 weeks after letting you know. If we still haven’t completed your application after three months, we delete it as well. If you give us permission to keep your application, we keep it for one year at most, in case a place opens up later. You can withdraw that permission at any time by emailing info@bsosporty.nl.

Booking a trial day

Booking a free trial day through our trial day page? Then we receive your name, email address and phone number, your child’s name and age, the time slot you choose and anything else you tell us. We only use this data to plan the trial day, send you a confirmation and get in touch with you if anything changes.

Your booking comes into our own admin system, not to an external service; anything you tell us in the comments field is stored encrypted. We delete your booking 4 weeks after the trial day. If you then sign up your child, we keep your details with your registration.

Staying in the loop

Signing up through Stay in the loop or the pop-up on the website? Then we receive your name, your email address, your home address and, if you fill them in, your phone number and your child’s first name. We use them to send you the occasional email with news about Sporty and to post you the Sporty goodies. We only do this with your permission, and only after you’ve confirmed your sign-up with the link in the email we send you.

Your details are stored in our own admin system, not with an external service. If you don’t confirm your sign-up, we erase your details after 14 days. Otherwise, we keep them until you unsubscribe, and for 24 months at most. We only need your address for the goodies: we erase it 30 days after we’ve posted them. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email; we then erase your details straight away.

Signing up your child

Signing up your child through Join the club? Then we receive the details you fill in: for your child, their name, initials, gender, date of birth, school and school year (groep), their sports club (if you fill it in, so we can take your child there), the membership you’d like, the days and start date, and anything you tell us about, for example, allergies; for you, your name, initials, gender (if you fill it in), address, email address, phone number and how you found us; and, if you wish, your partner’s name and contact details. We use this data to get in touch with you, discuss the options and complete your registration. Signing up doesn’t commit you to anything.

Whatever you fill in on the form is remembered by your browser on your own device, so you can carry on later. We don’t receive it, and it’s erased as soon as you send your registration; you can also erase it yourself with "Start again". Using a shared computer? Then send your registration in one go, or choose "Start again" before you leave. We also count how often each step of the form is reached, without cookies and without knowing who you are, so we can improve the form.

Your account in Mijn BSO

After we’ve got to know each other, you’ll get an account in Mijn BSO, our secure parent portal (in Dutch). In it, we store:

  • your details: name, email address, phone number, address and your contact preference;
  • your child’s details: name, date of birth, school, who is allowed to collect your child, and any special information you enter yourself, such as allergies or medication;
  • the days your child attends, and when your child arrived and was collected;
  • the messages between you and us, for example to let us know your child is ill;
  • observations by our staff about your child’s development (our child monitoring system). You can see the observations we share with you in your account;
  • short working notes from the team about your child, for example about what helps when your child is having a hard time. These aren’t shown in your account, but if you ask to see your data, you’ll receive them;
  • photos our staff take of your child;
  • your invoices and the details of your direct debit mandate.

We use this data to arrange the childcare, take good care of your child, communicate with you and invoice for the childcare. Only the staff of your child’s group and the management of the BSO can access this data. A parent or carer can only see the children they are linked to.

Only someone with parental authority over your child can make decisions on their behalf, such as changing details, giving permission or making a privacy request. We check this once, for example with an extract from the Dutch custody register (gezagsregister). We can give other carers, such as a grandparent, access to things like photos or messages, but they can’t make any decisions.

Photos of your child

You decide whether we share photos of your child: group photos too, only photos with just your child in them, or preferably no photos at all. Until you’ve told us, we don’t share any photos of your child. A photo with only your child in it is shared with you alone. A group photo is shared with the parents of all the children in it, but only if all those parents have given their permission. You can change your choice at any time in your child’s details; if you allow less than before, you can also have photos that have already been shared removed. We never put photos of children on the website or social media without separate, written permission. When we save a photo, we remove hidden data from it, such as the location.

Payments

For direct debits and online payments, we work with Stripe, a payment provider with an office in Ireland. You give the mandate on a secure Stripe page: through your own bank (iDEAL | Wero) or with your account number. We only store the account holder’s name and the last digits of your account number; the full details are held by Stripe. See also Stripe’s privacy policy.

Security

Mijn BSO only works over a secure connection. Everyone has their own account. Our team always logs in with two-step verification (a code from an app on their phone); you can switch this on for your own account too. We store sensitive data, such as allergies, observations, messages and photos, encrypted. We store passwords in such a way that nobody can read them back, and after a number of failed login attempts, logging in is temporarily blocked.

We keep a log of who views, changes or exports which data. Every day, we make an encrypted backup of all data, which we delete again after 30 days. We use one cookie to keep you logged in; that cookie is needed for Mijn BSO to work and disappears when you log out or close your browser.

How long do we keep your data?

We don’t keep your data any longer than necessary. Questions sent through the contact form are kept until your question has been dealt with. For the data in Mijn BSO, the following retention periods apply, and we apply them automatically:

  • observations and working notes about your child: until 3 months after your child has left us;
  • your child’s other data, such as special information and who may collect them: until 3 months after leaving;
  • photos: 12 months;
  • messages and sickness reports: 24 months;
  • your account and contact details: until 3 months after leaving. A registration that doesn’t lead to childcare is deleted after 6 months;
  • a booked trial day: 4 weeks after the trial day;
  • invoices, payments and the days they are based on: 7 years, because the law requires it;
  • the log of who viewed or changed which data: 24 months.

Anything we delete also disappears from our backups within 30 days.

Cookies and external services

We use Google Tag Manager, a service from Google. It lets us see how the website is used and how well our ads work, for example with Google Analytics. We only do this if you give your permission in the cookie notice. If you say no, nothing loads from Google and Google doesn’t place any cookies.

If you do give permission, Google places cookies (such as _ga) and receives information about your visit, such as which pages you view, your IP address and which device and browser you use. Google may also process this information outside the European Union; Google takes part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. How long the cookies last is explained in Google’s overview of cookies. Mijn BSO doesn’t use Google Tag Manager.

We remember your choice for 12 months in a cookie of our own (sporty_cookiekeuze), so we don’t have to ask on every page. You can change your choice at any time via Cookie settings, also at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your permission, we remove Google’s cookies from your browser.

The fonts are on our own server, so your browser doesn’t connect to Google for them.

The map on the Locations and Contact pages is an image on our own server (made with map data from OpenStreetMap). So nothing loads from Google. If you click the map or "Plan your route", Google Maps opens in a new tab. From that moment on, Google’s privacy policy applies.

Do we share your data with others?

No. We never sell your data, and we only share it with parties that help us provide our service (such as the form service, our hosting providers Vercel for the website and Vimexx for Mijn BSO, and Stripe for payments), with Google if you allow cookies (see above), or when the law requires us to. We sign a data processing agreement with these parties.

Your rights

You have the right to see your data, have it corrected or deleted, and take it with you in a file. You can also object to the use of your data or ask us to temporarily restrict its use. Got an account in Mijn BSO? Then you can download your data yourself and submit a request under Mijn privacy (in Dutch). You can also do this on behalf of your child, if you have parental authority. Or send an email to info@bsosporty.nl. We respond within one month.

Sometimes we can’t delete data because the law requires us to keep it, such as invoices. If that’s the case, we’ll explain it to you.

Not happy with how we handle your data? Let us know and we’ll look for a solution together. You also always have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
